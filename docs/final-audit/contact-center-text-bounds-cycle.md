## Implemented behavior

New inbound text is capped at 4,096 UTF-16 units without splitting a Unicode scalar. Database-invalid NUL and lone surrogates become U+FFFD; other database-valid controls remain unchanged. New summaries use the same scalar-safe bounds. SMS replies additionally replace XML-invalid scalars. Family labels are capped before assembling a complete fallback, and concierge replies remain within 320 units. The candidate boundary independently caps new replies at 1,599 units before validation and fingerprinting, below the documented provider limit. [Twilio Message verb](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/twiml/message), [PostgreSQL JSON constraints](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/datatype-json.html).

Stored version-one inputs, fingerprints, projections and read/archive markers are immutable. A historical queued reply of at least 1,600 units is held with the existing conditional suppression transition and the new output reason `unsupported_content`; reserved replies are never reopened. Stored receipt validation remains strict. Legacy null-summary reconstruction and the authorized status reader use identical scalar-safe prefixes.

The candidate cancellation signal now reaches provider completion, with checks before and after asynchronous setup and completion. Ignored or late results cannot persist after the receipt deadline. This does not guarantee durable capture before a candidate exists.
